⚡ What even is this

This is not just a calculator. This is the final stage of evolution of computing devices. While others write 300 lines of React with WebAssembly and a Rust backend just to add 2 + 2, here everything runs on pure Python + tkinter, as the ancestors intended.

You open a window. You press buttons. You get a result. No dependencies. No npm. No 400 MB node_modules. No Docker. No Kubernetes. No blockchain.
🔥 Why it's hardcore
Regular calculator	TERMINAL-CALC
Asks for updates	Works since 2005
Requires internet	Works in a bunker
200 MB of dependencies	0 MB. Standard library.
Crashes on division by zero	Shows a messagebox and lives on
Buttons don't respond to mouse	They do. And to keyboard too.
Light theme, burns your eyes	Dark theme, spares your retinas
💀 Features that make Senior devs cry

    ➕ Addition — works

    ➖ Subtraction — works

    ✖️ Multiplication — works

    ➗ Division — works (and doesn't even crash on division by zero, because we handle exceptions like adults)

    🧹 C — clears everything. Like cleaning in life. Harsh, but necessary.

    ⌫ Backspace — deletes one character at a time. Meditative.

    🎨 Dark theme — because light causes pain

    ⌨️ Full keyboard support — Enter, Backspace, digits, operators

    🖱️ Adaptive grid — buttons stretch like your motivation on Monday
